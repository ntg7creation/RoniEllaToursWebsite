# Search, sharing and partner links

Open **עריכת SEO ותוויות / Edit SEO and topics** on the page. The last table column shows the value used in HTML. Clearing an override restores the displayed default. Open Graph and X use the social title, description and image. The topics field also supplies JSON-LD topics and article tags. Set image descriptions in the picker. Body images and guide portraits can have their own description for that placement.

The original WordPress URLs were checked against the [original sitemap index](https://roniellatours.com/sitemap_index.xml). The rebuild serves permanent redirects for matching Hebrew URLs and WordPress shortlinks. Public links, canonicals and the production sitemap use English paths. The Vercel review site intentionally blocks indexing while WordPress serves the business domain. The old domain must serve the redirects for existing backlinks to reach the new pages.

## Backlinks after the domain switch

1. Start with the guides’ own websites and tour partners that already mention the company. Give each partner the final HTTPS URL for the tour, destination or guide profile they describe. For existing printed material or older articles, the corresponding Hebrew URL continues to work through its redirect.
2. When a guide contributes an interview or a cultural article to a museum, tourism association or specialist publication, provide the related itinerary or article as a source. Use a descriptive link such as “Uzbekistan Silk Road itinerary” in the language of the publication.
3. Keep a short record of the referring page, destination URL, contact, and date checked. Verify that each published link reaches the intended page in one hop. Track referral visits or enquiries through the company’s existing reporting when available.

Google recommends direct permanent redirects when URLs move and keeping them for at least a year. Keep frequently used old tour links available for returning visitors. Search and AI discovery use crawlable page text and structured data. The production site also publishes a plain-text directory at `/llms.txt`.
